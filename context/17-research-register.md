# Research register and evidence limits

Research date: 2026-11-15. The user asked for source research and links; durable URLs are included in the specialist documents.

## Method

Initial research used three parallel specialist agents and a coordinating architect. The expanded phase added twenty distinct focused research-agent assignments, executed in waves within the same concurrency limit, plus specialist follow-up dossiers. The web search/open tool returned empty payloads, so source content was fetched directly from official documentation, project repositories, publisher sites and product sites using HTTP requests. A successful HTTP status alone was not treated as enough: only readable relevant content supports the specific findings. Challenge pages, inaccessible content and uninspected bibliography entries are marked accordingly. Competitor descriptions are public product claims, not independent performance tests.

No authenticated provider call, exhaustive literature review, book download, corpus ingestion, predictive experiment or software benchmark occurred. Book candidates are not a claim to possess their full contents. No copyrighted book text was reproduced in this package. Research was broad enough to design a pilot, not to assert global novelty or that all competing apps lack citations.

## Evidence classification

**Inspected primary source:** the researcher read the relevant official material. Supports only the attributed factual claim, not all claims about a product.

**Candidate bibliography:** plausible work for domain review, with title/attribution and reason to investigate. Exact edition, translator, availability, accuracy and permissions may remain unresolved.

**User-reported behavior:** supplied FreeGG configuration and observed 410 on chat completions. Recorded faithfully, not independently reproduced.

**Design proposal:** stack selection, chunk budgets, architecture, UI, score thresholds and release gates. These are engineering judgments to test, not source facts.

**Unverified/inaccessible:** link identified but relevant content not readable. Such entries cannot justify a definitive technical or rights claim.

## Further research before building or launching

1. Domain lead chooses a coherent technical corpus and reviews actual pilot editions, translations, exceptions and rule prerequisites.
2. Rights reviewer records explicit ingestion, storage, derived-index and excerpt-display permissions for each edition and operating jurisdiction. No assumption that internet availability implies reuse permission.
3. Run provider compatibility and data-handling review. Confirm route, reported model identity, accepted parameters, quotas, retention/deletion practices and terms. A name matching a model does not prove the service supplies that model.
4. Verify pinned package releases, licenses and thread-safety; benchmark chosen encoder and parser on representative data.
5. Validate independent reference charts and historical timezone edge cases.
6. Interview users and audit competitor reading outputs with consent to test whether visible provenance improves trust calibration. Avoid marketing claims of uniqueness without this research.
7. If investigating predictive validity, design a separate preregistered, consented study with suitable controls and independent analysis. Retrieval metrics and satisfied-user feedback do not answer that question.

## Research maintenance

Every future source-derived change records URL, access date, relevant fact, whether content was actually inspected and affected decision. Time-sensitive hosting quotas are checked again before spending or deployment. Prefer publisher/project documentation over scraped summaries; preserve bibliographic metadata and permission records separately from any downloaded content.
